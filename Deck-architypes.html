Deck Architypes

Here you can find a list of deck architypes for both casual and competitive play. Each architype has a brief description and a list of recommended cards to include in your deck. Whether you're new to the game or a seasoned player, these architypes can help you build a strong and effective deck.

Control

Control decks focus on disrupting the opponent's strategy and gaining card advantage. They often use counterspells, removal, and card draw to control the game.

Aggro

Aggro decks aim to win quickly by playing low-cost creatures and dealing damage to the opponent as fast as possible. They prioritize speed and efficiency.

Midrange

Midrange decks strike a balance between aggro and control, using efficient creatures and spells to adapt to the game state. They can switch between aggressive and defensive strategies.

Spell Slinger

Spell Slinger decks focus on casting a high volume of spells efficiently. They often utilize card draw and mana acceleration to maintain a consistent stream of threats.
